# Test 00053

The power operator: right associative, tighter than unary minus.

## Expected results

- `pow1`: `2 ^ 10` = 1024.0
- `pow2`: `2 ^ 3 ^ 2` = 512.0 (right to left: 2 ^ 9, not 8 ^ 2 = 64)
- `pow3`: `-2 ^ 2` = -4.0 (-(2 ^ 2))
- `pow4`: `(-2) ^ 2` = 4.0
- `pow5`: `4 ^ 0.5` = 2.0
- `pow6`: `2 ^ -1` = 0.5
- `pow7`: `10 ^ -2` = 0.01
- `pow8`: `5 ^ 0` = 1.0

## Sign-off

- Expected results: derived by hand (see above), not taken from a simulator.
- Tolerances: the defaults (absolute 1e-7, relative 1e-4).
- Backends: roadrunner, copasi, opencor (agree within the tolerances; admitted 2026-10-09 by the suite runner).
